# Legitimate-release source package

The implementation and source ledger are in
[tools/release_foundations/README.md](../tools/release_foundations/README.md).
It covers AWW, REC, REL, SBK, SER, SHI and TER, preserving their original entry
contracts. It introduces no forced release, war, border, core, population or
automatic combat grant. Existing saves are adopted without physical rewards;
new eligible releases and future paid choices have separate once receipts.

The machine-readable country registry now identifies guarded release owners,
including AWW's preserved personnel/non-core-capacity liability. Its fourteen
footprint cases, four BBA/MTG projections and optional maintenance recipes remain
conditional source coverage. Unknown release shapes have a bounded capital and
local-maintenance fallback, with wider network coverage explicitly open.

The accepted 61-active-start packet, its UST manpower debt and BLK 309 geographic
exception remain unchanged. Finance continues to use the authoritative P 1 public
account/state-output owner. Three optional office representations fill only
genuinely new regimes without an eligible authored leader; no saved individual
is replaced or recast.

The package is for source review. Native playtesting remains paused, publication
and installation synchronization remain separate actions, and the normal
installation/user saves remain untouched. Completed offline results are recorded
under `docs/evidence/release-foundations`; they do not establish engine acceptance.
