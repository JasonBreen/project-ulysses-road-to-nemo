# Composed active-world implementation

This review packet composes 61 active starts from four physical owners: fifty
WorldStarts profiles, five World Foundation profiles, two Anea profiles and four
retained Legacy profiles. The 69-history registry records those actual owners,
seven dormant release contracts and the unresolved PLH placeholder. It does not
claim universal sustainability or native acceptance.

The primary implementation, provisional budgets and preserved liabilities are
documented in `WORLD_STARTS_CALIBRATION.md` and `WORLD_STARTS_ACCEPTANCE.md`.
The source partition reads 244 selected BBA/MTG starting cases, with separate NSB,
FATO-context and La Resistance evidence and release gaps. Existing country
histories apply starting setup only to new campaigns. Borders, cores, province
rasters and inherited population remain unchanged.

The complete composed source snapshot passed 36 commands. Ten affected review
checks subsequently passed on their separately recorded commit, strengthening
the actual WorldStarts suite to 25 tests, startup chronology to 16 regressions
and 1,120 actual selected cases, reconstruction to 16 runtime/10 source-art tests,
and effective-provider validation to 41 regressions. Exact results and payload
hashes are under `evidence/world-starts/composed/` and `review-followup/`.
Earlier independent reviews and their bounded correction recheck remain in
`independent/` and `independent-recheck/`; they are not a new final-head review.

The final parent also includes the decision-row repair. The six additional
replacement-note rows legitimately expand its actual consumer inventory from
549 to 555 variants, and the old oversized bindings would affect 73 rather than
67 variants. Regenerating its review report with the owning renderer records
all 555 with zero current source-bound failures; the original artwork snapshots
are unchanged. The illustrative comparison images do not need replacement,
because those six notes use the same repaired Anea row sprite. The generated
report, manifest and real-consumer regressions remain authoritative for scope.

One inherited event-loaded Ustio OOB also lacked its final closing brace. The
exact original normalized prefix and existing veteran formation remain intact.
Its actual caller and repaired payload are checked by the existing compatibility
gate. The separate missing civil-war militia OOB remains documented in
`evidence/world-starts/ustio-oob/README.md`; no unsupported force grant is added.

The narrow `.gitattributes` exception preserves whitespace in immutable captured
authoring inputs under `tools/world_legacy/baseline/`. It does not exempt
delivered mod source. The shared logistics owners, legacy-army receipts and
once-only country-history loads remain independently checked.

Native testing and desktop control remain paused. Ustio's established manpower
debt, Belka's sealed inherited component, manual small-budget replacement
rotations, fuel/trade capacity limits, extra-DLC behavior and the seven release
foundations remain explicit in their acceptance records. Source execution,
primary metadata, CI, offline pictures and native play are separate evidence
tiers. No normal installation, profile, playset, save, merge or release was
changed by this implementation.
