# Reconstruction continuations: authoring draft

This source batch implements the approved additional budgets for North Point
(18), Recta (18), Sapin (12) and Gebet (12): **60 definitions**, including exclusive
alternatives. `content.py` contains the sixty individual titles, descriptions,
art directions, policy tradeoffs and twenty service objectives. They are an
authoring draft: no mod exports, completed validation or delivered-count claim
exists yet. The release ledger remains at 234/1,028 delivered until the complete
compiled package and evidence are ready.

All new domestic choices, coefficients and procedures are provisional gameplay.
The established governments, office leaders, borders and existing war/aftermath,
faction and project owners remain authoritative. The official chronology is
context, not a source for invented domestic policies. The existing Chronicle and
campaign ledgers supply North Point's hosting context, Sapin/Gebet's aftermath
and the inherited Rectan settlement. Recta remains an emergent country under
its legitimate existing release contract; this package never releases it.

## Runtime design to implement

- One `ac_rc` owner, current **and** original host identity, country-scoped weekly
  dispatch. No iteration of the whole world once per country.
- Preserve existing tree IDs, definitions and completion. Add shared imports in
  bounded owner sections; put new definitions in a separate owned file. Existing
  generators must preserve the new import sections in both authoring orders.
  No country-history setup, new starting grants or forced tree replacement.
- Date-gate branch entries from 2006. Each branch follows its recorded existing
  assessment/legacy focus. Domestic conclusions have no upper date cutoff;
  late completions after 2013/2020 remain useful. No expired preparation payout.
- Six definitions per branch: register; two mutually exclusive enduring
  policies; operational protocol; completed-service review; enduring settlement.
  Default 35 days, major policy/settlement commitments 70 days. A shared
  prerequisite group permits either policy. Preserve each policy's identity in
  its improved concluding variant, including its continuing countervailing cost.
- The register and operational protocol unlock the two distinct services.
  Reviews require an actually completed service period, not an unlocked flag.
  Each focus therefore has a policy/capability/action or narrative consumer.
- One funded service active nationally, 90-day duration and a 90-day cooldown
  after completion. Explicit 35 PP and four institutional-capacity points at
  authorization; fiscal `ac_econ_can_pay_programme`/`ac_econ_pay_programme` when
  ready, with full/experimental/disabled routes. No private fiscal stock writes.
  Use engine-supported `custom_cost_trigger`/`custom_cost_text` and guarded
  explicit PP payment, so repeated private callbacks cannot charge or pay twice.
- Apply the named temporary service modifier for its actual period. On
  completion, record one completed service for that branch, remove active
  state and start the cooldown. Interrupted periods retain costs and delivered
  time, stop future benefits and never qualify as a completed review. Use
  documented annexation, capitulation and war hooks for rapid interruptions.
  Subjects retain useful domestic work; foreign eligibility is separate.
- Forbid civil-war inheritance of new policy/service spirits with the documented
  trigger-block form. Current/original identity guards leave copied flags inert.
- Optional civilian information/standards offers may follow operational
  protocols. Both sides revalidate existence, independence, war/capitulation
  and current/original identities. A single pair owner prevents duplicate
  cross-offers. Refusal/expiry/ineligible partners leave the domestic service
  and review reachable. No faction membership, access, borders or authoritative
  war flags are written. Consider FCU–NPT, REC–GEB, SAP–OSE and domestic fallbacks.
- Briefs, branch responses and final assessments are authored national narrative
  events with once-only delivery; do not invent official people or cameos.
- Decision categories show actual current service, costs, cooldown/progress,
  completed reviews and the chosen policies. No additional economic dashboard.
