## Runtime and services

`ac_rc` owns its flags, progress, completion records and messages. The real P1
public APIs own programme affordability/payment and institutional capacity.
Authorization consumes 35 PP and four capacity, plus the applicable shared fee
(two legacy units or 0.002 billion) only when that fiscal owner is ready. The
full, experimental and disabled routes are exercised against the actual owners,
without mocked readiness, balances, quote or payment functions. No new annual
commitment duplicates this already-paid programme fee.

Twenty services operate for ninety days, nationally one at a time. Their temporary
spirits end at day90; the next weekly callback records successful completion and
starts a ninety-day cooldown. That observation can be up to six days later.
Until completion is recorded, an interruption cancels the active period. Spent
costs and delivered time remain, future benefits stop, and no completed-service
credit is granted. Verified engine hooks cover annexation, subject annexation,
immediate capitulation and added war relations. Weekly observation handles invalid
ownership/status as a fallback. Rapid known-hook interruption and restoration is
regressed. No unobserved transient engine state or native hook ordering is claimed.
Subjects can perform useful domestic work.

Current and original host identity are both required. Cloned records are inert;
new spirits use the documented `allowed_civil_war = { always = no }` form.
New initialization sets only missing owner-bookkeeping fields. It grants no fiscal
seed, old preparation reward, technology, history setup or physical assets. Late
chains and national conclusions remain eligible after 2020. There are no new
post-2020 historical dispatches or wars.
