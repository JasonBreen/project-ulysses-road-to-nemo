# Paid programme eligibility

The Usean partner, reconstruction and superpower services use the public fiscal owner. A fee-free compatibility route applies only when `ac_econ_enabled = no`. An enabled but unready account must not authorize work, spend political/institutional resources, reserve national capacity or issue a completion receipt. This covers missing identities, missing initialization and invalid identity receipts without rewriting or reseeding the financial account.

The production correction changes one eligibility clause in each of three generated trigger files. The existing public payment, completion, interruption, cooldown and delivered-work owners are unchanged. Already funded services retain their existing future completion rules; the correction governs new authorization. Full and experimental modes pay their existing 0.002b/2FU programme fee. Fiscal-disabled campaigns retain the established political and capacity costs.

The actual-owner regressions exercise all **81 scopes**: partner29, reconstruction20 and superpower32. Four unready-account fixtures per scope verify the public enabled/ready observations and assert unchanged complete country state after a rejected start. Existing suites also cover successful payment in all three fiscal modes, interruption and duplicate accounting. No readiness or monetary substitute is added to the interpreters.
