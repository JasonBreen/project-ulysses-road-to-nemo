Every statement was split into claims, paraphrased, checked against the
official sentence by an independent reviewer and corrected where the reviewer
found an addition, an omission or a changed name or date. Coverage was traced
in the repository: ledger rows, inherited events and focuses. The record keeps
the paraphrase, the named entities, the coverage with its evidence, the
disposition, the conflicts with mod content and the unknowns; it stores a
hash of each official sentence, never the sentence itself.

Rules, from [CANON_POLICY.md](../CANON_POLICY.md): only these pages are canon
tier here; `XX` and seasons stay unknown; a date is an anchor, never a forced
outcome; named people gain no biography or office beyond the sentence;
squadrons, ships and aircraft stay text; places are never mapped to states.

## Not found in the official pages

- **Capitals.** Named: Directus (Ustio), Farbanti (Erusea), Gracemeria (Emmeria),
  Griswall (Aurelia) and, outside the horizon, Theve (the FCU in 2029). Not named:
  the Osean capital on these pages (Oured appears only in the column above), and
  the capitals of Belka, Yuktobania, Estovakia, Sapin, Gebet, FATO and every other
  country.
- **A country list or map.** No official page lists the countries of Strangereal
  or draws their borders; the 1991 territorial transfers name buyers only as
  "FATO", "neighboring eastern nations" and Osea.
- **Office-holders and terms.** Named in office: Harling (48th President of Osea
  from 31 December 2004), Nikanor (Prime Minister of Yuktobania), Robert Sinclair
  (FCU President, 2003), Richard Payne and Stateson (FCU, 1996), Howards (Osean
  foreign minister, 1991), Isaac Arensky and Antonina Koznick (Estovakian foreign
  ministers), Robert Tyler (FCU Army, 1995–1996) and Diego Navarro (Leasath's
  commanding officer, removed in December 2020). No start or end of a term beyond those
  dates; no Belkan, Erusean (before the monarchy) or Usean head of state.
- **Forces.** No unit strength, order of battle or equipment list; squadrons and
  ships are named only as participants.
- **Causes and outcomes not stated.** Who attacked at Cape Landers or Port Saint
  Hewlett, who destroyed the satellites in 2019, who killed Harling, the terms of
  the Lumen treaty beyond "favoring Osea", the terms of any ceasefire.
- **Exact days.** 2003 / SUMMER, 2015 / 11.XX, 2019 / XX.XX and every 2020 entry.
