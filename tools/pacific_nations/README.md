# Pacific national continuations

This owner adds the approved remaining 44 Osean and 42 Yuktobanian definitions in
independent country modules. Delivery status follows each completed component's
evidence; an approved count or a source file is not delivered/native acceptance.
The national components are Osea 44 and Yuktobania 42. CPW and the two new story
packets remain separate work until their own coherent handoffs.

All procedures, scene incidents, indices, policy alternatives and calibration are
original provisional gameplay. The official context stays in `sources.json`.
No leader terms, starting borders, history setup, units, technologies, factories,
project capabilities, war outcomes or protected private continuity are added.

## Work and ownership

The national continuation consumer is `ac_snc_*`; new country nodes use
`OSE_snc_*`/`YUK_snc_*`. It preserves both Osean election trees, the Yuktobanian
tree and all existing focus IDs. The registered import block is composable with
the existing expansion, PWI, reconstruction and SNP generators. Strict legacy
preservation removes only the exact registered continuation IDs; unknown rows,
foreign nodes and changes remain errors. Ordinary work is 35 days; enduring policy
and settlement commitments are 70. All real conclusions remain eligible after 2020.

There is no new generic budget/register or parallel service reservation system.
Six individual lanes use their actual existing SNP parent and selected-lane
funded period. A protocol records the present request date and the next eligible
paid-start sequence for each lane. The review requires the matching period's
actual completion receipt, with its sequence at least that minimum, its start not
earlier than the request and its identity matching the current country. Historical aggregate
counts, old completion flags, another branch's work, cancellation at day 90 and
copied receipts cannot substitute for that evidence.

The SNP owner increments `<scope>_start_serial` only after a successful paid
start. It publishes four additive fields only when a real period finishes:
`<scope>_last_completed_started`, `_last_completed_on`,
`_last_completed_identity`, and `_last_completed_serial`. A period already
running earlier on the same calendar day cannot satisfy the later request.
Initialization does not backfill them. Legacy in-flight work can finish under
its existing contract but publishes sequence zero. The consumer
never changes SNP shares, support, reservations, completion dates or payments.
Its funded work therefore retains the actual three-unit Osean schedule or the
Yuktobanian 100-point allocation/two-office bound, real PP/capacity costs, and
the existing P 1 fee in full/experimental modes. Disabled fiscal play keeps its
political/capacity costs. No fee is charged again by this review consumer.

Immediate annexation, capitulation and subject-annexation hooks clear pending
current requests/messages. Weekly checks repair eligibility and expiry. A rapid
return cannot revive an old request. Renewing it costs 10 PP and 2 institutional
capacity through the actual political owner and sets a fresh date and minimum
paid-start sequences; the matching
SNP service still pays/reserves its ordinary work. Domestic subject work remains
eligible. Wrong current/original tags or a copied initialized identity receive
no reward. Old date-only requests are closed without changing completed choices,
receipts, money or reservations; their explicit renewal remains available.
Repeated initialization/reload grants no resources or old scenes.

Four eight-node lanes retain two separate policy choices: the early civil or
protected priority, then a public or signed staff return after actual work.
The smaller continuity/account lanes retain their own conclusions. Enduring
and ninety-day narrative spirits all have separate favorable and unfavorable
modifiers. Settlements improve the chosen policy benefit 20 percent while keeping
its actual cost. No foreign reply, war declaration or war conclusion is required.

Fourteen national scenes per country provide an opening, six hearings, six
assessments and a concluding account. Their 60 day reply windows, persistent
choices and missed-invitation callbacks are separate from the funded service.
An unanswered hearing does not block the national focus chain. The public
prewar-verification read API can require an actual recorded answer; ignoring it
does not create consent. Expired/annexed/cloned/replayed options cannot grant
the temporary spirit or change a choice again.

The subsequent CPW package may read the explicit earned-verification/channel
triggers. It owns its calendar/escalation/outcome records separately, and must
never write these national accounts, faction membership or Arkbird state.
Faction/guarantee presence alone is not a declaration veto. A pre-existing or
expanded war requires an explicit external observation path. No scripted peace
is part of the approved first CPW owner; actual cessation controls ending news.

## Presentation and reproduction

Each focus has a distinct original three-subject composition. All new ideas,
categories, native 26 x 29 rows and fourteen briefing illustrations are packaged
with exact DDS/frame/alpha/hash/binding metadata and editable PNG masters.
The compiler uses the existing original glyph library, not engine pixels.
Galleries use shipped bitmap glyphs; PNG comparison checks exactRGBA/dimensions
rather than platform-dependent compression bytes. DDS remains byte-exact.
No watermark, invented official emblem or baked localization is included.

python -B tools/superpowers/build.py
