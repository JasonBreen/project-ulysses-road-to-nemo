# Canon and provenance policy

This private continuation uses four distinct source layers. Keeping them separate prevents a mod archive, a gameplay abstraction, and an official Project Aces fact from being accidentally treated as the same kind of evidence.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Official Project Aces / ACE COMBAT material.** Use official game material, official manuals or guides, official ACES pages, and publisher material for lore-sensitive facts: dates, borders, country and faction names, characters, named aircraft/equipment, and established setting relationships. The official [ACES web site](https://acecombat.jp/) describes the shared Strangereal setting and the series' connected historical backdrop; it also identifies ACE COMBAT Zero as *The Belkan War* in its official archive.
2. **Sam's established Joker 1 continuity.** This is an approved user-authored creative layer. It may guide future content when Sam's established storyline is compiled and cited to an agreed private source, but it must be labeled `user-authored continuity`, never `official canon`.
3. **The April 5, 2024 Project Ulysses archive and current repository.** These are mod provenance. They show what the original team attempted or preserved, not what Project Aces officially established. Archive content may be retained, adapted, or flagged as conflicting without being treated as canon proof.
4. **Provisional gameplay abstraction.** Balance scaffolding, placeholder names, inherited engine IDs, and mechanics created to make HOI4 playable are allowed only when explicitly labeled. They must not be written as lore statements.

## Required ledger fields

Every lore-sensitive addition or correction should record:

- the claim or identifier;
- source layer (`official`, `user-authored`, `mod provenance`, or `provisional gameplay`);
- an exact primary citation or private-source reference;
- the affected files/IDs;
- whether the claim is implemented, adapted, disputed, or pending review; and
- any conflict with the archive or another source.

## 2065 rule

The campaign may be engineered to run through 2065-12-31, but a date alone does not establish a canon event. Do not invent future history to fill the horizon. Content through 2065 must be classified as official-source-backed, Sam's user-authored continuity, inherited mod provenance, or provisional gameplay abstraction. The repository must state which of these has actually been authored and runtime-tested.
