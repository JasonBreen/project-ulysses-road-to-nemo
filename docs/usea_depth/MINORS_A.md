# Usea Depth: minor states A (Chopinburg, Platania, Ferustia, Albard, Dorondo, Scandia, Bulgurdarest)

Generated by `python -B tools/usea_depth/minors_a/build.py`; do not edit by hand. Native status: not tested in HOI4. Native acceptance remains under #8.

No official source covers any of these seven states. Their geography, names, resources and starting spirits are **mod provenance** (`history/countries`, `history/states`, `common/ideas/ferustia.txt`); everything authored for them here is **provisional gameplay**. Official Chronicle anchors are used only as dates, and every national response to them is provisional. No new named people: each provisional government is an unnamed office with a generated card. No place is mapped to a state. No `transfer_state`, cores, claims, borders, wars, annexation or faction changes exist in this package, and nothing writes an `ac_ucw_*` flag.
