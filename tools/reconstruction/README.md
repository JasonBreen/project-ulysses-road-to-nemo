# Reconstruction continuations: RC60

This additive source package contains **60 focus definitions**: North Point 18,
Recta 18, Sapin 12 and Gebet 12. Counts include mutually exclusive alternatives.
Ten six-definition branches connect an inherited entry to a register, one of two
enduring policies, an operational protocol, an actual service review and a
standing settlement. The existing tree IDs, focuses, events and outcome owners
are retained. There are no history, leader, force, state or map changes.

All new domestic procedures, incidents, dialogue, policy values and artwork are
original provisional mod content. Existing campaign and canon ledgers provide
context; these additions claim no newly established official facts. Recta still
requires its existing release contract. No new diplomatic acceptance is required.

## Runtime contract

Entries open from 2006. North Point retains `NPT_exp_civil_resilience`; Recta
retains `REC_exp_recovery_assessment`. Sapin and Gebet accept their recorded
legacy entry, with a peaceful domestic fallback only while the authoritative
Belkan postwar contract has never begun. An existing valid legacy chain is never
bypassed or auto-completed. Work and final assessments remain useful after 2020;
there is no expired preparation payout or retroactive historical event.

Each country has one service slot. Twenty distinct services provide named
ninety-day temporary modifiers. Authorization spends **35 political power,
four institutional-capacity points and the shared programme fee when fiscal
readiness permits**. The actual P1 owners implement capacity and payment. The
full economy fee is 0.002 billion; the experimental legacy fee is two units.
Fiscal-disabled play retains its political and institutional opportunity costs.
No private fiscal stocks are written, and the new owner adds no permanent
budget commitment for a fee already paid.

Completion requires ninety uninterrupted days, then a ninety-day cooldown.
Timed benefits stop at day ninety. Completion is recorded at the next weekly
observation, so its record and cooldown can begin up to six days later. War,
capitulation, annexation, loss of land or player cancellation ends an active
period without review credit or refund. Delivered time is retained. Verified
annexation, capitulation and war hooks prevent rapid interruption/restoration
from reviving a period before weekly detection. Unobserved status changes are
not claimed to have native coverage. Subjects retain domestic services.

The owner checks current and original host identity. Copied clone records are
inert; spirits forbid civil-war inheritance. National services never change
access, faction membership, borders, authoritative war outcomes or projects.
North Point's guarded-access policy explicitly preserves existing undertakings.
No bilateral offer subsystem is introduced by this package.

The twenty policy alternatives have specific benefits and continuing costs.
Their final settlements improve the selected benefit by twenty percent while
retaining the same cost. Reviews require a genuinely completed service, including
when opening or branch messages were unanswered. Twenty-eight original national
events provide opening briefs, branch responses, branch assessments and national
conclusions. Sixty-day reply reservations, exact-once answers and explicit
unanswered callbacks prevent stale replies and invented past choices. Lost
messages do not block the focus/service chain.

## Source and reproducible artwork

- `content.py`: the sixty individual definitions, twenty policies and services.
- `narrative.py`: twenty-eight original events and conditional callbacks.
- `build.py`: guarded runtime, localization, manifests and bounded imports.
- `build_art.py`: original vector compositions using the existing Ulysses glyph
  library. Pillow **11.3.0** is required for byte-exact packaging.
- `assets.json`: provenance, dimensions, one-frame metadata, alpha extrema,
  hashes and sprite bindings for **198 DDS textures** and 74 editable PNG masters.
- `gallery/index.html` and four country sheets: offline artwork review.
- `engine_contracts.json`: metadata-only primary 1.19.4.1 modifier/hook evidence.

The DDS package contains sixty distinct 96×96 focus compositions, ninety-six
75×75 spirits, twenty-four 26×29 decision-row icons, four 100×100 categories and
fourteen 640×260 event illustrations. Decision rows resolve their actual
unprefixed `icon` fields through `GFX_decision_` bindings. Shared framing and
related policy variants are deliberate; no engine/DLC pixels, remote artwork,
portraits, watermark or baked localization text is imported. The event artwork
is procedural briefing illustration, not a claim of hand-painted scenes.

The older expansion generator now removes only its own support definitions and
uses bounded sections; it no longer truncates everything after an open-ended
marker. Disposable fixtures verify both generator orders and preserve foreign
trailers byte-for-byte. The PWI importer remains additive. Continuous panels are
moved clear of old nodes and inside the expanded focus canvas. Every resolved
node and caption is checked; native viewport and input behavior remain pending.
