# Add four Usean partner campaigns and Erusean starting forces

San Salvacion, Amber, Ugellas and Delarus gain persistent national programmes
with 72/60/48/48 focuses, 54 decisions and 24 national choice events. Their finite
service records, ongoing opportunity costs, preparation handoff and settlements
use the existing Continental War owner and the actual shared fiscal/institution
APIs. Old saves can adopt the national trees after existing work finishes without
new historical forces or preparation rewards. Each current/original national
host has source selection weight 40 against its regional fallback's 5.

The candidate preserves the current partner military/industrial/logistics rosters
from the World dependency and integrates the additive Erusean foundation, including
its reviewed land, air and naval rosters and component logistics. Erusea retains
its individual 28 CIV/23 MIL/11 dockyard placements and 16 inherited ports. Existing
leaders and office representations remain in place. The 69-country registry keeps
its fiscal/institution metadata and explicitly classified remaining support gaps.

Immediate recipient/sender interruption now prevents an offer or accepted service
from reviving after rapid annexation, capitulation or an observed subject
transition. Both new-war participants are checked; unrelated wars remain eligible.
Pending Amber slots return once, while paid costs and accepted allocations remain
spent. Temporary service spirits also forbid unpaid civil-war inheritance.

Validation: 30 national tests; 4 real-owner integration tests covering 29 scopes in
three fiscal modes; 20 World/Erusean tests; macro, ledger, institution, force,
legacy, faction, campaign and repaired-screen checks; generated output and
`--strict-all`; full static/reference wrapper. Commands and limitations are in
`docs/USEAN_PARTNER_INTEGRATION.md` and its retained evidence.

The 311 original DDS assets reproduce with all 228 focus compositions distinct.
No source image or PNG was changed by integration. The roadmap records 228 new
partner definitions and 234 delivered additions including the previously merged
six FCU focuses, leaving 794; the regional 36 are separate. Squadron stories remain
a separate integration. Native gameplay, real saves, rendering, AI pace and
balance remain untested while native testing is paused.
