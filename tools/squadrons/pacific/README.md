# Pacific personal campaigns

`ac_sqp_*` owns two new original supporting stories: Osea's **Second Frequency**
and Yuktobania's **The Names on the Board**. Each has20 core choices, four
recurring people and three personal conclusions. Archives, cast cards and
conclusions are counted separately from the40 core chapters. The original six
campaigns retain their six-row registry,120 scenes,24 people,282 total events,
78 selected originals and139 delivered DDS.315 old content/runtime/media files
are pinned to their exact pre-extension Git blobs in `legacy_contract.json`.
PNG/DDS also retain their exact raw bytes. Never recapture that contract to make
a failing preservation check pass.

The old compiler's optional row/initializer/scene-map parameters preserve all
default output. The schema admits the2020 horizon only for the explicit OSE/YUK
extension identities. Separate metadata files permit the Osean component to
land coherently before the Yuktobanian component. Existing six-host assertions
are not changed to generic minimum counts.

## Personal consequences and admission

The Osean story follows Rhea, Malik, June and Sana as private favors become
public obligations: rivalry, June's hand injury, rest, family commitments and a
loss outside the four. The Yuktobanian story follows Irina, Anton, Daria and Lev
as a board of names becomes a question of who may answer: Anton's hearing
strain and new teaching role, crew respect, reporting and a different personal
loss. All four principal people in each story remain alive. Neither story
appoints a government leader, imports a canonical cameo or displaces established
protagonists. Incidents, dates, dialogue and personnel are authored fiction.

Every choice has two bounded persistent relationship changes and a unique flag;
eighteen later chapters per campaign may recall earlier choices. Privacy and
loyalty can help cohesion while weakening a public account. Credible disclosure
can cost intimacy. The initial scores are50/50; trusted requires both at least65,
otherwise cohesion below45 is fractured and the remaining state is compromised.
All three endings are reachable, including a trusted path using both buttons.
The trusted ending gives a single future365-day2% air experience benefit after
an actually read scene. No choice pays national money, equipment, factories,
manpower or retrospective preparation.

Admission requires the exact current/original country, its own numeric identity
receipt and the existing `ac_sq_stories` preference. A newly loaded account after
January1991 skips the opening. Expired earlier acts are filed without applying
choices; a new admission after2020 is closed without historical grants. The
fourth act stays open for an already admitted story to conclude after2020.
Pending scenes have30-day reservations, one exact chapter position and one
delivery snapshot. Expiry and known annexation advance only the missed scene,
never create its choice. A stale answer cannot repeat or replace a later choice.
Copied identity receipts cannot advance or display someone else's archive.
Static category admission is independent of startup receipts; live visibility
enforces them. Refreshing UI triggers does not advance or pay anything.

## Read-only integration and presentation

Ordinary scenes need no aircraft. The training, weather-contact and uncertain-
return variants require peace, noncapitulation, deployed aircraft and deployed
air personnel. The wartime flight perspective reads the actual counterpart war
relation and current air capability. The unpublished CPW component is not a
dependency and no war owner is installed by either story. Optional existing
outcome flags are read only. Expanded fighting, reciprocal restraint, unilateral declaration
withholding and capitulation have distinct useful continuations. The scientific
variant requires completed earlier Arkbird cooperation and its actual Yuktobanian
partner receipt; it never asserts a surviving operational platform. Delivered
context and callbacks are frozen for the archive even if national conditions
change. No per-wing sortie, combat loss or aircraft availability is observed.

The26 selected original masters and five superseded edit references are retained
in the separately reviewed Pacific art source component. Only the exact26 names
are admitted alongside the immutable original78; prefixes are not exemptions.

cards and sixteen scenes use full-source200x160 contain. Three endings per

screens use their reviewed center cover to1920x1080; their192x144 thumbnails use

stories,47 DDS bind45 sprites; eight portrait exports are not national leader

The actual-script harness includes the real SNC, SNP, political and fiscal
libraries. Only engine observations, event delivery and personal arithmetic are
adapted. Decimal/deepcopy execution, source geometry, asset closure and CI are
not native parser, save serialization, UI, fixed-point arithmetic, battles,
natural progression or AI-pacing acceptance. Native/desktop testing is paused.

The source runner pins clean raw inputs before and after every invocation,
archives superseded receipts, writes a failed current receipt after exceptions,
and selects this interpreter on PATH for child PowerShell checks. Generate the
dependency profile explicitly with `capture_dependencies.py --write-profile`
before committing; validation checks it without rewriting source. Current
event, decision and economy consumers are audited separately from the inherited
global dependency backlog.
