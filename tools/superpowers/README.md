# Superpower national programmes: first implementation slice

This work-in-progress source slice owns **96 additional definitions**, forty-eight
for Osea and forty-eight for Yuktobania. The approved final additions remain
92/90; a later slice still owes 44/42. Do not count this draft as delivered until
its actual scripts, narratives, artwork, owner integration and checks pass.

The 1991 foundations and 2006-2013 institutional continuations are additive to
the established national trees. Osean alternative trees receive identical owned
imports; Yuktobania retains its existing intervention, reform and Arkbird
branches. Existing IDs and completed choices stay authoritative. This slice
does not implement or claim acceptance of the Circum-Pacific War.

## Mechanical direction

Osea uses a **federal commitment register**: a limited schedule allocates staff
between domestic services, accountable readiness and external undertakings.
Published commitments require renewed funded service; protected appropriations
provide readiness with enduring civilian or political costs. New public and
security support observations describe the adopted policies, not invented
parties, elections or officeholders. Their visible value must have actual
eligibility/cost/readiness consumers rather than becoming decorative scores.

Yuktobania uses an **allocation docket**: civilian and command-controlled shares
sum to one hundred. Policy shifts change who can authorize technical staff,
transport capacity and inspection periods. Neither share mints cash or equipment.
Civilian audit and command protection have useful consequences and continuing
costs. Existing command-reform flags and the Arkbird project owner remain
authoritative; these procedures cannot silently reverse those choices.

Each of sixteen branches contains six individually written focuses: establish a
record/unlock a service, choose one of two enduring policies, authorize a specific
operational scope, review a genuinely completed service, and retain a useful
settlement. Major commitments take seventy days; other focuses take thirty-five.
The political choice, funded service and review must change actual capability or
available action. Do not implement repeated political-power/factory rewards.

Use P1 public fiscal and institution APIs. Fixed political/administrative costs
remain when fiscal simulation is disabled. One named paid period cannot create
parallel unpaid benefits, bypass physical production, manufacture stockpiles or
claim unsupported per-wing telemetry. Every timed scope needs a national slot,
progress, cancellation, cooldown, exact-once completion and retained spent costs.
Services affected by war require explicit useful wartime/domestic alternatives;
do not strand a national conclusion behind bilateral consent.

No bilateral rewards should be added until the owner has an actual finite-state
offer/accept/refuse/expire contract, revalidation at every transfer, exact-once
payment/delivery accounting and interruptions at rapid status changes. Optional
cooperation can read the existing Arkbird partnership without changing it.

Initialisation is country/original-tag scoped and once-only. Old saves receive
no historical preparation grant, office change, factory or army. Domestic
programmes can start late, but expired historical invitations are skipped.
Conclusions remain reachable after 2013 and after 2020. Civil-war clones must
not inherit benefits or original-host receipts. Avoid country-wide weekly N²
iteration; use the existing country-scoped observation conventions.
